PromptNova values your privacy. By using this website and its tools, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the website.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect limited information when you use PromptNova, including:

  • Text prompts, keywords, topics, or content ideas that you enter into our tools.
  • Basic technical data such as browser type, device type, approximate location, pages visited, and usage activity.
  • Information you voluntarily provide if you contact us, such as your name, email address, or message.

You should not enter sensitive personal information, passwords, private financial details, confidential business data, or any information that you do not want processed by AI services.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Generate AI content based on your prompts.
  • Improve website performance, features, and user experience.
  • Monitor and prevent misuse, abuse, spam, or technical issues.
  • Respond to support requests, feedback, or contact messages.
  • Maintain security and reliability of the website.

3. AI Content Processing

PromptNova uses third-party AI or API services to generate content. When you submit a prompt, that text may be sent to an external AI service for processing. The generated output is based on your input and the AI model response.

We do not guarantee that AI-generated content will always be accurate, original, complete, or suitable for every purpose. You are responsible for reviewing, editing, and verifying generated content before publishing or using it.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

PromptNova may use cookies, local storage, analytics tools, or similar technologies to improve functionality, remember preferences, analyze traffic, and understand how users interact with the website.

You can disable cookies through your browser settings. Some website features may not work properly if cookies or browser storage are disabled.

5. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information only in the following situations:

  • With service providers that help operate the website, backend, AI processing, analytics, hosting, or security.
  • When required by law, regulation, legal process, or government request.
  • To protect the rights, safety, property, or security of PromptNova, users, or the public.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information from unauthorized access, misuse, loss, or disclosure. However, no website, server, or internet transmission is completely secure. Use the website at your own discretion.

7. Data Retention

We retain information only as long as necessary for website operation, legal compliance, security, analytics, or support purposes. AI processing providers may have their own data retention practices, which are governed by their respective policies.

8. Third-Party Links and Services

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or use third-party services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, terms, or content of third-party websites. Please review their policies before using them.

9. Children’s Privacy

PromptNova is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

10. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain uses of your personal information. You may also request that we stop contacting you if you have previously reached out or subscribed to communications.
